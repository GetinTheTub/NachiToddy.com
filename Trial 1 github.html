Our Story

Crafted the
Old Way

Nachi Toddy has been a gathering place for the people of Malacca — a spot where neighbours linger over fresh-tapped palm toddy and the morning light stretches long through the trees.

Every glass is drawn from palms tapped before sunrise, bringing you a drink that is alive — slightly sweet, gently fermented, utterly fresh. We take no shortcuts, add no flavourings, and rush nothing.

This is toddy as it was meant to be: honest, unhurried, and shared among friends.

"Tapped at dawn, shared by noon — that is the rhythm of toddy."
— Nachi, Founder
