Frequently Asked Questions

What is Coconut Toddy?

Coconut toddy (also known as kallu or palm wine) is a traditional, milky-white, alcoholic beverage created by fermenting the sweet sap collected from the budding flower of a coconut palm tree. It has a slightly sweet, tangy taste, typically ranging from 4% to 8% alcohol content.

Types of Toddy

Mix tree: Juice combined from young trees, 1-2% alc, generally sweeter (NO MORE PRODUCTION)

Single tree: Juice from single source of older tree kept in 1-2 bottles/tree, 3-4% alc, generally lesser sweetness & more coconut essence/woody flavor.

Our single tree toddy can keep 3-4 weeks in fridge (longer period creates stronger taste & alcohol).

What are the Health Benefits?

Good for detoxing the tummy because of the high enzymes, high probiotics. Can cool the body/remove body heat, good for blood circulation, low fats+high carb so it's healthy to consume daily.
